Game Day Primer

The Missouri Tigers play their home games on Faurot Field at Memorial Stadium in Columbia. The field is named for Don Faurot, the longtime Missouri coach credited with inventing the Split-T formation in 1941.

Above the north end zone sits the Rock M, a giant white "M" built from rocks by Mizzou freshmen in 1927. Mizzou also claims to have started college Homecoming back in 1911, when alumni were invited "home" for the game against Kansas.

That Kansas game was part of the Border War, one of the oldest rivalries in college football. After Missouri moved from the Big 12 to the SEC in 2012, the series went on hiatus.

Cheering on every Touchdown is Truman the Tiger, the school's mascot, dressed in black and gold.

Coach Eli Drinkwitz, hired in December 2019, led the Tigers to an 11-2 season in 2023, capped by a 14-3 win over Ohio State in the Cotton Bowl.
